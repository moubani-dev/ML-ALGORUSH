## 💡 About the Project

ExamRisk AI is an AI-powered exam preparation tool that helps students identify the gap between what they think they know and what they can actually demonstrate.

The student enters:
- Subject
- Topics
- Confidence level

Gemini then generates a short diagnostic test. After the student answers, the application calculates their actual performance and compares it with their confidence.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 AI-generated diagnostic questions
- 📊 Confidence vs. performance analysis
- ⚠️ Exam risk detection
- 🎯 Topic-wise risk identification
- 📚 Personalized revision recommendations

↓  
Confidence Gap  
↓  
Exam Risk Report

## 🎯 Problem

Students often spend time revising topics they already understand while overlooking topics where they have false confidence.

ExamRisk AI helps identify these hidden risk areas before the exam.
